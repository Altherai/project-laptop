Project Laptop is an independent gaming experiment documenting the process of reaching out to brands and building genuine creator collaborations.

01

The Project

Project Laptop documents a simple challenge: starting with an existing gaming setup and reaching out to companies to explore potential collaboration opportunities.

The goal isn't to pretend to be a large creator. The goal is to build something honestly, publicly, and step by step.

02

What We Can Offer

01 Authentic gaming content
02 Product exposure
03 Honest documentation
04 Long-term collaboration
03

What We Want To Create

Gaming videos, gameplay, experiments, product experiences and behind-the-scenes content.

We are still developing the content format, which means brands can become part of the journey rather than simply appearing at the end.

04

Collaboration

We are currently looking for gaming, hardware and technology companies interested in supporting an independent creator project.

Hardware support, product testing, sponsorships and other collaboration formats are all welcome for discussion.
