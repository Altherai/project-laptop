01

The Experiment

Can a small creator build a hardware partnership from zero?

Project Laptop is an independent experiment by BugsBunny6251, a small gaming YouTube channel.

We are documenting the process of approaching gaming and technology companies and exploring whether a genuine collaboration can be built.

There are no inflated numbers, guaranteed results or promises of unrealistic exposure.
